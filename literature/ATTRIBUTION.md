# Attribution and source-reading boundaries

This is an import of the recorded source comparison, not a new literature search. Follow the exact passages and limitations in the [full contribution review](../archive/research-handoff-2026-10-08/prior/REVIEW.md) and [final source check](../archive/research-handoff-2026-10-08/SOURCE_CHECK.md). External articles are linked, not redistributed.

| Source | Inherited contribution and boundary |
|---|---|
| Delacrétaz, Gorbenko, Wang, Zan, Zhabin, [Hydrodynamic tails in chaotic spin chains with quantum group symmetry](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20850v1) | Physical model, nonlocal symmetry, finite-size motivation, and nonlinear-charge improvement. It does not call its one-generator bound tight. Our spatial projection law is not its hydrodynamic exponent or a refutation of its simulations. |
| Moudgalya–Motrunich, [Hilbert Space Fragmentation and Commutant Algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10324) | Complete-commutant projection and quantum-group representation context. Different physical representations have different trace weights. No new general projection principle is claimed. |
| Hart, [Exact Mazur bounds in the pair-flip model and beyond](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00738v2) | Exact boundary/bulk memory and constrained-pattern methods in different representations. Those exponents do not directly transfer to this transverse spin-1/2 observable. |
| Li–Sala–Pollmann, [Hilbert space fragmentation in open quantum systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06918) | Strong-symmetry stationary commutants with coherent and dissipative evolution. Our bond-noise realization specializes this framework; the general mechanism is inherited. |
| Gorbenko–Zhabin, [Chaos in Systems with Quantum Group Symmetry](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23247v2) | Hecke construction of symmetry-preserving interactions. Its structural role is credited; the recorded inspection did not evaluate the same real-q transverse physical-trace law. |
| Koelink–Verding, [Spectral analysis and the Haar functional on the quantum SU(2) group](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9412225) | Al-Salam–Chihara recurrence and spectral measure. Its base is translated to z=q^-2; its Haar functional is not substituted for the physical spin trace. |
| Damanik–Simon, [Jost Functions and Jost Solutions for Jacobi Matrices, II](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502487) | Decay/analyticity of Jacobi Jost solutions. The moderate-window estimate and matching to the spin recursion are derived in the preserved proof. |
| Collins–Śniady, [Integration with respect to Haar measure](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0402073) | General Haar-integration attribution. The elementary moments used here are independently derived in the plateau note; the recorded final check did not inspect a specific equation from this article. |

The added implication is evaluation of the physical multiplicity trace and uniform finite-q spatial asymptotics. The finite-size recurrence, edge limit, restricted bulk limit, isolated-Hamiltonian excess, and noisy realization have distinct roles and claims.

The preserved reading records include failed full-text/PDF-image retrievals. Those failures are not evidence that a result is absent. No new source access or independent review is asserted by this import.

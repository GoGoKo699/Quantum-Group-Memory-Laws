# Discussion and contributions

For discussion or potential collaboration, contact Ruge Lin at [gogoko699@gmail.com](mailto:gogoko699@gmail.com).

Before proposing changes, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), the [claim boundaries](research/MODEL_AND_CLAIMS.md), and [verification instructions](VERIFICATION.md). Useful contributions identify a concrete proof step, a precisely comparable predecessor, or a reproducibility discrepancy at a named revision. Do not broaden the claim to a dynamical theorem, storage protocol, or different representation without explicitly identifying a new task.

Use a feature branch and a reviewable pull request. Preserve historical sources and reports; put new evidence outside the protected archive. Documentation-only changes must preserve scientific assertions and update only the relevant document-integrity metadata. Third-party papers should be cited, not relicensed or added to the archive.

# Model and results

The mathematical specification is [THEOREM.md](THEOREM.md). This record uses an open chain of $`L`$ physical spin-$`\tfrac12`$ sites, finite real deformation $`q\ge1`$, and the symmetric coproduct written there. $`X_i`$ is a Pauli operator, so $`C_i(0)=1`$.

The trace is the **ordinary physical** $`2^{-L}\mathrm{Tr}`$, not a quantum-dimension-weighted trace. The algebra is the full unital star-algebra generated by the quantum-group raising, lowering, and magnetization operators. Its physical multiplicities and irrep dimensions cannot be replaced by those of a different Temperley–Lieb representation.

## Central result

The full symmetry projection is evaluated exactly at finite size by a Clebsch–Gordan multiplicity-trace recursion. Its edge asymptotic holds for every fixed finite $`q>1`$. Its bulk equality, including amplitude and spatial profile, is proved for

```math
b(q)=\frac{q^{-1}+q^{-3}}{(1-q^{-2})^2}\lt 1,
```

with $`q`$ fixed and $`i_L/L\to x\in(0,1)`$. This is the sufficient domain $`q>q_0`$, not evidence of a transition at $`q_0`$. At $`q=1`$, the answer is exactly $`1/L`$ everywhere. The boundary-distance limit takes $`L\to\infty`$ first at fixed distance, then takes that distance large; it is not a uniform finite-chain interpolation.

The complete symmetry term is a lower bound on the finite-chain **infinite-time average** of an isolated symmetry-preserving Hamiltonian. No pointwise convergence of its finite spectrum is asserted. [The exact excess identity](PLATEAU_DICTIONARY.md) retains Hamiltonian-specific invariant operators and accidental degeneracies.
